## What is AssemblyAI?

AssemblyAI gives you APIs and SDKs for turning speech into text. You send audio or video and receive a transcript you can use in your application. Add speaker labels to tell who spoke, or process live audio for real-time experiences.

These docs are for developers adding transcription to tools for podcasts, meetings, interviews, or recorded calls. You only need basic Python knowledge to follow along.
